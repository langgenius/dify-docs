---
title: Find Apps That Need Repair
description: Find unavailable apps in Studio, repair their dependencies, and check them again
---

In **Studio**, set **Status** to **Unavailable**. On an app card, click the information icon beside **Unavailable** to open its dependency details.

The status checks the app's configured dependencies, such as models, tools, and knowledge bases.

<Info>
For Workflow and Chatflow apps, the status checks the editable draft even when a published version exists.

It does not tell you whether an app is published or whether its published version works.
</Info>

If the details report a missing model, open the app's configuration and select an available replacement.

If the check couldn't finish, reload Studio and try again before treating a dependency as broken.

After saving a repair, reload Studio to check the status again. Then run the app's actual task, even if the status is **Available**.
