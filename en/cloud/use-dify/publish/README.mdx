You've built something great in Dify. Now let's get it to your users. Every Dify application becomes available in multiple ways automatically—choose what works best for your situation.

## Start with Web Apps

Your fastest path to sharing is through web apps. These are generated automatically when you create any application and work immediately without setup.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Hit Publish">
    Click **Publish** in your app to activate the latest version.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Copy the URL">
    Find your web app link on the app's **Access Point** tab.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Share immediately">
    Send the link to users, or [restrict access to approved networks](#ip-access-control) before sharing.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Tip>
Web apps work on any device and automatically adapt to screen sizes. No app store approvals or installation required.
</Tip>

## How Publishing Works

When you publish an app, Dify creates a web app and API endpoint with your latest configuration:

- **Web apps** update immediately with new features and responses
- **API endpoints** serve the latest model and workflow configurations
- **Website embeds** (which display your web app) automatically reflect all changes
- **MCP servers** provide access to current app capabilities

<Warning>
Publishing replaces your live app with the current configuration. Users will immediately see changes in their next interaction.
</Warning>

## Limit Access to Trusted Networks <Badge color="blue">Professional</Badge> <Badge color="blue">Team</Badge> {#ip-access-control}

Keep your app accessible only from networks you trust, such as your office network or specific servers, even if its link is shared.

You can set up and manage IP access control under **Access Point** > **Access Control** in your app.

<Info>
Only workspace owners and admins can create, edit, or delete IP policies. Editors with permission to manage an app's access points can configure its Access Control using existing policies.
</Info>

### Set Up Access Control

1. Under **IP Policy**, select an existing policy. You can reuse the same policy across apps in your workspace.

    To create a new policy, click **Create an IP policy**.

    Enter a **Name** and add the addresses you want to allow to the **Allowlist**:

    - To allow the network you're currently using, click **Add it** next to your current IP address.
    - For other networks or systems, ask whoever operates them for the public IP addresses they use to access your app. Your current IP address may differ from theirs.

    You can enter individual IPv4 or IPv6 addresses, or a range in CIDR notation. Adding a range allows requests from every IP address in that range.

    Click **Create**, then select the new policy in **Access Control**.

2. Under **Apply to**, turn on the access points you want this IP policy to protect, then click **Save**. Unselected access points remain unrestricted by this policy.

To check your setup, try accessing the app through a selected entry point from an allowed network and one outside the allowed range.

### Manage Access Control

- **Change allowed addresses**: Click **Edit**, then the **Manage IP policies** icon next to **IP Policy**. Select a policy and check **Used by**, as changes affect all apps using it. Update the **Allowlist** and click **Save**; changes take effect without republishing.

- **Change the policy or protected access points**: Click **Edit**, update your selections, and click **Save**. Saving also re-enables paused Access Control.

- **Pause or resume**: Turn off **Restrict by IP address** and confirm. IP restrictions stop applying, but your settings are kept. Turn it back on to resume.

- **Delete a policy**: In **IP Policies**, open the policy’s **…** menu and select **Delete**. Deletion is permanent and removes IP protection from all apps using the policy.

<Warning>
Your apps lose IP protection if your workspace no longer has an active Professional or Team subscription. Your IP policies and access point selections are kept. To use IP protection again, renew or upgrade to Professional or Team.
</Warning>

## Publishing Best Practices

Before you share your app, ensure you've configured these settings:

- **App description** - Helps users understand what your app does
- **Icon and branding** - Makes your app recognizable and professional
- **Access controls** - Decide whether to restrict access to approved networks
- **Rate limits** - Protect your app from overuse (especially important for API access)

<Check>
All publishing methods use the same app configuration. Set it once, publish everywhere.
</Check>
