---
title: IP Access Control
description: Restrict app access to trusted networks with reusable IP policies
---

Keep your app accessible only from networks you trust, such as your office network or specific servers, even if its link is shared.

You can set up and manage IP access control under **Access Point** > **Access Control** in your app.

<Info>
IP access control is available on the Professional and Team plans.

Only workspace owners and admins can create, edit, or delete IP policies. Editors with permission to manage an app's access points can configure its Access Control using existing policies.
</Info>

## Set Up Access Control

1. Under **IP Policy**, select an existing policy or click **Create an IP policy**. Policies can be reused across apps in your workspace.

    For a new policy, enter a **Name** and add the addresses you want to allow to the **Allowlist**:

    - To allow the network you're currently using, click **Add it** next to your current IP address.
    - Ask whoever manages the networks or systems that need access to your app for their public IP addresses.

    You can enter individual IPv4 or IPv6 addresses, or a range in CIDR notation. Adding a range allows requests from every IP address in that range.

    Click **Create**, then select the new policy in **Access Control**.

2. Under **Apply to**, turn on the access points you want this IP policy to protect, then click **Save**. Unselected access points remain unrestricted by this policy.

To check your setup, test a selected access point from an allowed network and one outside the allowed range.

## Change Allowed IP Addresses

Click **Edit**, then the **Manage IP policies** icon next to **IP Policy**. In **IP Policies**, select a policy, update its **Allowlist**, and click **Save**.

Changes affect all apps using the policy and take effect without republishing.

## Change the Policy or Protected Access Points

Click **Edit**, update your selections under **IP Policy** or **Apply to**, and click **Save**.

Saving also re-enables paused Access Control.

## Pause or Resume Access Control

Turn off **Restrict by IP address** and confirm. Turn the switch back on to resume.

While paused, IP restrictions stop applying, but your settings are kept.

<Warning>
IP protection also stops if your Professional or Team subscription becomes inactive. Your settings are kept. Renew or upgrade to either plan to use IP protection again.
</Warning>

## Delete an IP Policy

In **IP Policies**, open the policy’s **…** menu and select **Delete**.

Deletion is permanent and removes IP protection from all apps using the policy.
