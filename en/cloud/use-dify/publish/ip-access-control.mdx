---
title: IP Access Control
description: Restrict app access to trusted networks with reusable IP policies
---

Keep your app accessible only from networks you trust, such as your office network or specific servers, even if its link is shared.

Available on <Badge color="blue">Professional</Badge> and <Badge color="blue">Team</Badge>.

You can set up and manage IP access control under **Access Point** > **Access Control** in your app.

<Info>
Only workspace owners and admins can create, edit, or delete IP policies. Editors with permission to manage an app's access points can configure its Access Control using existing policies.
</Info>

## Set Up Access Control

1. Under **IP Policy**, select an existing policy. You can reuse the same policy across apps in your workspace.

    To create a new policy, click **Create an IP policy**.

    Enter a **Name** and add the addresses you want to allow to the **Allowlist**:

    - To allow the network you're currently using, click **Add it** next to your current IP address.
    - Ask whoever manages the networks or systems that need access to your app for their public IP addresses.

    You can enter individual IPv4 or IPv6 addresses, or a range in CIDR notation. Adding a range allows requests from every IP address in that range.

    Click **Create**, then select the new policy in **Access Control**.

2. Under **Apply to**, turn on the access points you want this IP policy to protect, then click **Save**. Unselected access points remain unrestricted by this policy.

To check your setup, try accessing the app through a selected entry point from an allowed network and one outside the allowed range.

## Manage Access Control

- **Change allowed addresses**: Click **Edit**, then the **Manage IP policies** icon next to **IP Policy**. Select a policy and check **Used by**, as changes affect all apps using it. Update the **Allowlist** and click **Save**; changes take effect without republishing.

- **Change the policy or protected access points**: Click **Edit**, update your selections, and click **Save**. Saving also re-enables paused Access Control.

- **Pause or resume**: Turn off **Restrict by IP address** and confirm. IP restrictions stop applying, but your settings are kept. Turn it back on to resume.

- **Delete a policy**: In **IP Policies**, open the policy’s **…** menu and select **Delete**. Deletion is permanent and removes IP protection from all apps using the policy.

<Warning>
If your workspace no longer has an active Professional or Team subscription, IP protection stops, but your policies and access point selections are kept. To use IP protection again, renew or upgrade to Professional or Team.
</Warning>
