Your personal account holds your profile, language preferences, and login credentials. Your role and permissions within the workspace are set by the workspace Owner or an Admin.

## Account Setup

The administrator account is created during installation, using the email and password set at deploy time. Additional accounts are added by invitation from a workspace Owner or Admin.

## Profile Management

Update your profile information in **Settings → Account → Profile**.

**Profile picture**: Upload a custom avatar. This replaces the default initials-based avatar.

**Display name**: How you appear to other workspace members. Choose something that helps teammates identify you.

**Email address**: Your primary login credential and unique identifier.

## Language and Interface

**Display language**: Available languages include English, Simplified Chinese, and Traditional Chinese. This setting affects interface elements but not your application content.

**Change language**: Click your avatar, choose **Language**, then select your preferred language.

## Login Methods

Sign in with your email address and password. The credentials are the ones set when the administrator account was created at install time, or provisioned later by an Owner or Admin.

<Tip>
  On Dify Enterprise, you can sign in with OAuth or SAML single sign-on instead of a password. [Contact sales](https://share-na2.hsforms.com/14-09ff5HS92Sh4m3f4yrcw40s9fk) to learn more.
</Tip>

## Personal Access Tokens

A personal access token lets scripts, CI jobs, and coding agents act as you through [`difyctl`](/en/cli/get-started), without signing in. Any member can create tokens on their **Account** page.

### Create a Token

Click **Create token** and give the token only what its job needs, since anyone who gets hold of it can do everything it allows.

- **Expiration**: Choose a period that covers how long the job will run, rather than **Never**. This cannot be changed later; if the job outlasts it, [replace the token](#change-replace-or-delete-a-token).
- **Resources**: The apps the token can run.
- **Manage workspaces**: The workspaces where the token can set up integrations, such as models and tools.

For the `difyctl` commands that **Resources** and **Manage workspaces** each allow, see [Choose the Account and Scopes](/en/cli/personal-access-tokens#choose-the-account-and-scopes).

Within its scopes, the token can do only what your role allows in each workspace. A change to your role, or your removal from a workspace, applies from the token's next use. If your account is disabled, your tokens stop working.

Once it's created, [give the token to `difyctl`](/en/cli/personal-access-tokens#give-the-token-to-difyctl).

### Change, Replace, or Delete a Token

- **Change the name or scopes**: New scopes apply from the token's next use, and the job keeps the same token.
- **Replace before it expires**: Duplicate the token, switch the job to the new one, then delete the old one.
- **Delete**: The token stops working at once, though a run already under way still finishes.

## Security

Use a strong, unique password and rotate it regularly. Don't share login credentials with others.
