Once apps depend on a knowledge base, its content keeps changing under them: sources sync, files go out of date, a model gets swapped. Check the **Overview** tab now and then, and fix what it flags from the **Source** and **Document** tabs.

## Watch the Overview Tab

You'll find four kinds of flags under **Needs attention**, each with its own fix:

- **Source data is stale**: the source has never synced successfully, or its last successful sync was more than 7 days ago. 

    In the **Source** tab, check the source's error if it shows one, then click **Sync now**.

- **Document processing failed**: the latest version of a document couldn't be indexed. Retrieval keeps serving the last good version, so nothing is missing, but the update isn't in yet. 

    In the **Document** tab, hover over the status to see the reason, then retry, or fix the source content and sync again.

- **Query quality needs review**: a question from your apps came back with weak or no evidence. It's waiting as a bad case under **Quality**. 

    See [Bad Cases](/en/3.14.x/use/knowledge-base/test-and-tune#bad-cases) for how to work through it.

- **Model configuration is incomplete**: a required model is missing, or the models haven't been validated yet because no document has been indexed. 

    Set any missing model under **Settings** > **Retrieval**; validation happens when the first document is indexed.

The **Query outcomes** chart shows every question your retrieval tests, your apps, and API callers sent over the selected period, sorted by how retrieval went:

- **No evidence** means nothing came back at all. 
- **Low confidence** means chunks came back, but every one fell below the score threshold, or the best one scored too low to trust. 
- **Answered** is everything else.

Each low-confidence or no-evidence question also opens a bad case, so the chart tells you how often retrieval falls short and **Quality** tells you on which questions.

## Keep Sources Fresh

Click **Sync now** on a source in the **Source** tab when you can't wait for its next scheduled run.

Removing a source deletes only the connection. Its documents stay in the knowledge base but stop refreshing, so delete them separately if you no longer want the content.

## Update Documents

To update an uploaded file, delete the old document and upload the new version. Uploading a file with the same name adds a second document instead of replacing the first.

When content is only temporarily out of date, disable the document instead of deleting it. It leaves retrieval until you enable it again.
