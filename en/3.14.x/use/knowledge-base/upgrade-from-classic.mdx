Upgrading builds a [new knowledge base](/en/3.14.x/use/knowledge-base/overview) from a [classic one](/en/3.14.x/use/knowledge/readme) and leaves the classic one exactly as it is. Your apps keep answering from the classic knowledge base until you switch them over, so you can review and test the new one at your own pace.

The new knowledge base serves Workflow and Chatflow apps, the [Service API](/en/3.14.x/develop/api/guides/knowledge-base), and the [Dify CLI](/en/3.14.x/develop/cli/reference/knowledge). Chatbot, Agent, and Text Generator apps stay on the classic one.

<Info>
    Only ready-to-use knowledge bases can be upgraded.
</Info>

## What Carries Over

The upgrade brings your content into the new knowledge base and indexes every document again there. Sections, summaries, and chunks are built fresh and won't match the classic ones, so [review them](/en/3.14.x/use/knowledge-base/review-indexed-documents) before connecting the knowledge base to an app.

Everything else comes across with it:

- **Documents**: uploaded files are uploaded again, and Notion pages and crawled websites come in through [sources](/en/3.14.x/use/knowledge-base/create-knowledge-base#connect-a-source) the upgrade creates for them.

- **Metadata**: the fields and every document's labels, so filters in your apps keep working.

- **Retrieval settings**: Top K, score threshold, and the embedding and rerank models. If either model is no longer available in your workspace, the workspace default takes its place.

- **Access**: the knowledge base's visibility (set in **Settings** > **Permissions**) and API access if it was enabled. Existing API keys don't cover the new knowledge base, so create a new key after upgrade.

- **Basics**: the name, description, icon, and tags.

The reasoning model and [retrieval depth](/en/3.14.x/use/knowledge-base/retrieval-settings#retrieval-depth) have no classic counterpart. The model starts as the workspace default, and depth starts at Fast.

## Upgrade a Knowledge Base

<Info>
Before you start, check that:

- You have edit permission on the classic knowledge base.
- The new knowledge base is enabled on your deployment. If it isn't, **Upgrade** doesn't appear in the menu. See [Environment Variables](/en/3.14.x/deploy/advanced-configuration/environment-variables) for the setup.
- It's a ready-to-use knowledge base. Custom and external knowledge bases can't be upgraded.
- For Notion pages, the Notion data source integration is connected in **Integrations** > **Data Source**.

</Info>

In **Knowledge**, on the **Classic** tab, open the knowledge base's menu and click **Upgrade**. The upgrade runs in the background, so you can leave the page. The new knowledge base is created on the **New** tab under the same name.

Expect minutes to hours depending on size. Indexing consumes tokens from the reasoning and embedding models, as with any import. Warn whoever watches model spend before upgrading a large knowledge base.

Once the upgrade is complete, finish two things on the new knowledge base's **Source** tab:

- The sources the upgrade creates are set to manual sync, so give each one a schedule if the content should keep refreshing.

- A crawled website is recreated with only the pages already imported, so widen the crawl options if you want new pages picked up.

## Switch Your Apps

Before switching, [review the indexed documents](/en/3.14.x/use/knowledge-base/review-indexed-documents) and [test retrieval](/en/3.14.x/use/knowledge-base/test-and-tune) with the questions the app actually faces.

In your workflows:

1. Add a new [Knowledge Retrieval node](/en/3.14.x/use/nodes/knowledge-retrieval-v2) that searches the new knowledge base, and connect it where the classic Knowledge Retrieval node was.

2. Run tests, and remove the classic node once the app answers well from the new one.

Delete the classic knowledge base only when no app uses it anymore, including the Chatbot, Agent, and Text Generator apps that can't be switched.
