Your knowledge is indexed and retrievable, but the real test is whether it returns the right chunks for the questions your users will ask.

Run those questions against it before you connect it to an app:

- Keep the runs that come back right as golden questions, so later changes can't quietly break them. 
- Turn the ones that come back wrong into bad cases to fix.

## Test Retrieval

In the knowledge base's **Retrieval Test** tab, type a question and run it. Ask full questions the way your users would, not just keywords.

Run the same question at each [retrieval depth](/en/3.14.x/use/knowledge-base/retrieval-settings#retrieval-depth), and pick the one that returns the right chunks most consistently as your knowledge base's default.

You can also attach images to a question. See [Multimodal Retrieval](/en/3.14.x/use/knowledge-base/multimodal-retrieval) for which models make that work.

Every run stays under **Records**, so you can compare results before and after a settings change.

### Golden Questions

A golden question is a question paired with the chunks it should return. Together, your golden questions are the bar every later change has to clear.

When a run returns the right chunks, click **Keep golden question** under its results, tick the chunks that must come back, and choose whether all of them or any one of them has to return for the question to pass.

To add one without a run, open the **Quality** tab, enter the question, describe the chunk you expect, and click **Find evidence** to pick from the closest matches. If you already have a list of questions, import it there from a CSV file (up to 1 MB).

<Info>
A golden question counts only once it has expected chunks. Until then it's a Draft, and [evaluations](#run-an-evaluation) skip it.
</Info>

### Bad Cases

When the right chunk is missing or ranked too low, click **Make bad case** under the results and pick the reason. Each points to a different fix:

- **Retrieval miss**: the chunk didn't come back at all.

    First [check how the document was indexed](/en/3.14.x/use/knowledge-base/review-indexed-documents).

    If it looks fine, widen the search with a deeper retrieval depth, a higher top K, or a lower score threshold under [Retrieval Settings](/en/3.14.x/use/knowledge-base/retrieval-settings).

- **Low score**: the chunk came back but ranked too low. The rerank model assigns the scores, and a different model may rank the same chunk higher.

Cases wait in **Quality** > **Bad cases**. After a fix, click **Replay** to rerun the question with the current settings. If the result is now right, click **Promote** to turn the case into a golden question and close it. **Ignore** closes a case you won't fix.

Bad cases also open on their own when a question from your apps comes back with weak or no evidence, so check the list once your apps are live, not only while you're testing.

## Run an Evaluation {#run-an-evaluation}

After changing a setting or model, go to **Quality** > **Evaluations** and click **Run evaluation**. It reruns retrieval for every golden question against the current settings, and passes when each one finds its expected chunks.

For a question that missed, click **Passages Found** to see which expected chunks didn't come back. Fix the cause the same way you would a bad case, then run again.
