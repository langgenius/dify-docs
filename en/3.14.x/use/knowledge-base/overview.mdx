An LLM's built-in knowledge stops at its training data. A knowledge base closes that gap: it turns your own content into context the model can draw on, so your apps answer from your data instead of guessing.

When a question arrives, retrieval finds the most relevant chunks of your content and adds them to the question as context. The LLM then answers from what it was given.

Create one knowledge base per domain, team, or use case, and attach it to the Dify apps that should answer from it. 

Each knowledge base can hold content from many places: websites, online documents (Notion, Confluence, Google Docs, and more), cloud storage, or files you upload directly.

<Tip>
To compare this new knowledge base with the classic one, or to upgrade, see [Upgrade from the Classic Knowledge Base](/en/3.14.x/use/knowledge-base/upgrade-from-classic).
</Tip>

## What You Can Build

- **Customer support chatbots** that answer from your help center and product docs. Sources stay in sync, so the bot never lags behind a release.

- **Internal Q&A portals** that answer employees' policy and process questions, using metadata to scope retrieval to the right region, department, or product line.

- **Research assistants** that investigate questions spanning a whole document set and assemble findings instead of returning matching snippets.

- **Coding agents and external tools** that read your team's accumulated knowledge through the [API](/en/3.14.x/develop/api/guides/knowledge-base) and the [Dify CLI](/en/3.14.x/develop/cli/reference/knowledge).

## How It Works

You'll do three things with a knowledge base: feed it content, test and tune its retrieval, and connect it to your apps.

### Feed

Content arrives as **sources** (live connections to websites, document platforms, or cloud storage that stay in sync on a schedule) or **files** you upload directly.

Every document is indexed automatically: 
- A reasoning model splits it into chunks and sections, writes a summary for each section, and notes the subjects each chunk mentions and how they relate, building up a knowledge graph across the knowledge base. 
- An embedding model then turns each chunk into a vector, so  questions can match chunks by meaning.

With vision-capable models, [images become searchable too](/en/3.14.x/use/knowledge-base/multimodal-retrieval).

### Test and Tune

Test with real questions to see what retrieval actually returns, and adjust the settings when results fall short. Save your best questions as golden questions, the benchmark the knowledge base must keep passing after every change.

Read more:

- [Test and Tune Retrieval](/en/3.14.x/use/knowledge-base/test-and-tune)
- [Retrieval Settings](/en/3.14.x/use/knowledge-base/retrieval-settings)

### Connect

Attach the knowledge base to Workflow or Chatflow apps, or let external agents and tools query it through the [Service API](/en/3.14.x/develop/api/guides/knowledge-base) and browse its documents with the [Dify CLI](/en/3.14.x/develop/cli/reference/knowledge). 

With metadata, you can scope each app's retrieval to specific documents.

Read more: [Use Knowledge from Your Apps](/en/3.14.x/use/knowledge-base/use-knowledge-in-apps)
